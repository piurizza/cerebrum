# Help

- [FAQ](faq.md): common questions about what Cerebrum does and doesn't do.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): symptoms and their fixes.

Still stuck? Search the
[issues](https://github.com/piurizza/cerebrum/issues), where known problems and
feature requests are tracked, or open a new one with
what you did, what you expected and what happened. Include the last lines of
`docker compose logs backend`, after removing anything private.
